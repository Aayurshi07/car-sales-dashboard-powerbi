# Car Sales Dashboard (Power BI + Python/SQL)

## 📌 Overview
An interactive car sales performance dashboard analyzing YTD sales, pricing 
trends, and dealer/company performance across the US. Data was sourced from 
Kaggle, cleaned and processed using Python and SQL, then visualized in 
Power BI for business reporting.

## 🎯 Business Problem
Sales leadership needed a consolidated view of car sales performance — 
tracking revenue trends, pricing shifts, top-performing companies/dealers, 
and regional sales distribution — to support data-driven decision making.

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills Used
- **Python & SQL** — data cleaning and preprocessing
- **Power BI** — interactive dashboard design, DAX measures, KPI cards
- **Data Visualization** — trend analysis, donut charts, geo-mapping

## 📊 Dataset
Car Sales dataset (Kaggle) — transactional car sales records including 
company, model, color, body style, dealer, and sales price.

## 🔍 Key Features
- **YTD KPIs:** Total Sales ($69.0M), Average Price ($27.98K), Cars Sold (2.6K) 
  with MTD comparisons
- **Sales Trend Analysis:** Weekly YTD sales trend visualization
- **Segmentation Views:** Sales breakdown by Body Style and Color
- **Geo Analysis:** Cars sold and average price by dealer region (map view)
- **Company Performance:** Ranked table of company-wise sales contribution 
  (% of total revenue)
- **Detail View:** Transaction-level drill-down (Car ID, Customer, Dealer, 
  Company, Model, Sales)

## 💡 Key Insight
Chevrolet, Ford, and Dodge together contribute over 20% of total YTD sales, 
highlighting concentrated revenue dependency on a few key manufacturers.
